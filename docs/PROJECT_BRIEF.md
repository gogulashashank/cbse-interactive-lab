# CBSE Interactive Lab — Project Brief

## Long-Term Ambition
An interactive CBSE science platform for Indian students in **Classes 9–12**, initially piloted with **40 students**. The platform aims to build conceptual intuition through structured inquiry, transparent mathematical models, and accessible interactive simulations.

## Current Milestone
Build **one complete local lesson**:
- **Title:** *"Why does a ramp make lifting easier?"*
- **Focus:** Exploring an ideal frictionless inclined plane to understand how reducing the required effort force trades off against moving over a longer distance, while total mechanical work against gravity remains constant.

## Current Exclusions
To keep the initial prototype focused and reliable, the following are explicitly excluded from the current scope:
- **No real student information** (no personal data collection or tracking)
- **No accounts** or authentication flows
- **No backend** services or external databases
- **No public deployment** (local execution only)
- **No claim of verified curriculum coverage** (all curriculum and scientific alignment remains marked as pending educator review)
